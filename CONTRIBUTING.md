# Contributing to wdx

Issues and ideas are welcome. Before implementing a change, open an issue describing the problem and agree on a design. Send one agreed step per pull request, with its tests and documentation.

Read the [contribution guide](https://srjn45.github.io/wdx/docs/project/contributing/) for the workflow, contribution licensing, and AI-assisted contributions, and [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md) for repository rules.

For vulnerabilities, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) rather than opening a public issue. Do not include secrets or confidential material in issues, commits, or pull requests.
